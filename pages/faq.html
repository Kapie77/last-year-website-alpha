Last Year - FAQ

Question

What platforms is this game being developed for?

Answer

The game is being developed for PC/Steam.

Question

Do you have plans for Mac/Linux/Console versions?

Answer

Our focus is creating only the PC platform right now. In the future we would like to branch out to other platforms but we are currently focusing our efforts on making the best possible PC experience.

Question

When will the game be available?

Answer

The Beta will be available mid-August 2017.

Question

Can I become a backer and support the game’s development before the release?

Answer

If you missed our Kickstarter crowdfunding campaign there will be another opportunity in the future to back the game for special rewards and a discounted price. Sign up to our newsletter to be notified when this will happen.

Question

Who is making this game?

Answer

The game is being developed by a team of industry veterans from Montreal that have worked on games including Assassin’s Creed and Far Cry series.

Question

How do I contact you?

Answer

Write us an email at lastyearthegame [AT] gmail dot com.
