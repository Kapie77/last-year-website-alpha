LOCATIONS

THE HIGHSCHOOL

Each location, such as East Side Highschool, is loaded with items like tools and weapons that you’ll scavenge for. Grab melee weapons from the shop class, health packs in nurses office, or build a bomb in the science class.

LOCATIONS

THE MALL

And in The Mall you and your friends will roam the halls as you try to survive the night. It’s complete with an arcade, department store, elevators and secret passages you’ll use to hide from or ambush your enemies.

LOCATIONS

THE SUBURBS

In the Suburbs you’ll explore garages, basements and tree forts as you look for ways to hide. When playing as the Killer you’ll lurk in the shadows, silently hunting your prey throughout a classic movie neighborhood.

LOCATIONS

THE CAMP

In Last Year you’ll sneak and slash your way through four classic movie inspired locations including Silver Lake Summer Camp. From the moment you start you’ll be on the run and you’ll need to use the environment to your advantage if you expect to stay alive.
